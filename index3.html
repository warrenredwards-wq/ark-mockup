ARK — The Itinerary

The Itinerary

"The first 30 days are about commitment, not results."

ARK tells her on Day 0:

"Your skin is beginning its first cycle. Trust the process. Your next Mirror Moment will show the truth."

She won't see change yet. She won't know how she's tracking. ARK keeps her honest. This builds trust — not through results, but through transparency.

The 12-Week Rhythm

01

Summer

Asia-Pacific · 90 days

High humidity. Intense UV. Barrier under siege. The formulation focuses on antioxidant defence, TEWL regulation, and mineral-based sun protection.

02

Autumn

Mediterranean · 90 days

Cooling air. The skin begins to pull inward. This is the repair window — ceramide restoration, lipid barrier rebuild, and the first signals of seasonal collagen response.

03

Winter

Northern Europe · 90 days

Cold, dry, unforgiving. Moisture stripped at every turn. Deep lipid repair takes priority. Occlusive layering, microbiome stabilisation, and intensive overnight recovery.

04

Spring

Southern Hemisphere · 90 days

The skin awakens. Cell turnover rises. Pigmentation becomes visible. The spring formulation capitalises on the body's natural resurgence — brightening, firming, renewing.
